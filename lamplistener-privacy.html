Lamplistener privacy

Effective September 27, 2026

Lamplistener connects only to the Jellyfin server you sign in to. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or a Lamplistener account, and the developer never receives your data.

Information stored on your device

  • Sign-in details: your Jellyfin server address, username, user ID, and the access token your server issues are saved in the iOS Keychain. Your password is sent to your server to sign in and is not stored.
  • Library and progress: book and chapter information from your server, and your listening position, are kept in the app’s local storage.
  • Downloads: audiobooks you download are saved on your device for offline listening.
  • Preferences: settings like theme, playback speed, and selected libraries.

Sharing and collection

Lamplistener talks only to the Jellyfin server you enter. It sends your sign-in request and your listening progress to that server so your place stays in sync across your devices. That server is run by you or whoever hosts it, and its own privacy practices apply. Lamplistener does not send your library, listening activity, or usage information to the developer or to any analytics or advertising service.

Purchases

The optional Offline Downloads unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you remove it. Use Settings > Sign Out to delete your saved sign-in details, remove downloads from within the app, or delete the app to erase all of its local data.
