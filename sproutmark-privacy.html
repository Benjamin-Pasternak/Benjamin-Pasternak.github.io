Sproutmark privacy

Effective September 21, 2026

Sproutmark stores your garden information on your device. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or a Sproutmark account.

Information you choose to add

Sproutmark can store your name, garden plans, plant and container details, notes, event history, and photos you take in the app. This information stays in Sproutmark’s local storage on your device.

Device permissions

  • Location: If you allow it, Sproutmark saves an approximate garden region and coordinates locally to provide seasonal context. You can remove the saved region in the app and revoke permission in iOS Settings.
  • Camera: Sproutmark uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo for a garden event.
  • NFC: Sproutmark reads and writes tags when you initiate tag setup or a manual scan. Tags contain an HTTPS link with an opaque local record identifier, not your notes, name, or photos.

Sharing and collection

Sproutmark does not send your garden data, location, photos, or usage information to the developer or to analytics and advertising services. Apple’s location and geocoding services may process a location request when you grant location access or search for a region. Opening an NFC web link may also cause your device to contact the website hosting that link before iOS opens the app.

Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it in Sproutmark or remove the app. Use Profile > Delete all local garden data to erase the local profile, containers, plants, events, notes, and photos managed by Sproutmark.
